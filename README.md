## The image

A 1024×1024 one-page newspaper front page for Tuesday 24 November 2026,
carrying 44 stories. Every event happened or was first reported on
24 Nov 2026 (any time zone). Events from 20–23 Nov appear only in the
red DATE CHECK box at the bottom, explicitly labelled as not today.

Layout, top to bottom:

- Navy banner: "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY" + "44 STORIES · 4 REGIONS ·
  EVERY ONE DATED TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026".
- Serif masthead: "Tuesday 24 November 2026", with a three-item
  standfirst (Ulleung shelter alert, the Swiss-office row, the Tokyo
  rally).
- Six-number stats row: 07:52 shelter alert on Ulleung, +3% Nikkei
  futures at reopen, 9 Champions League ties tonight, 10 NBA games
  tonight, $70bn US Treasury sale today, 8 U-17 World Cup games today.
- Colour region bar: ASIA-PACIFIC 20, AMERICAS 14, EUROPE 9, AFRICA 1
  (counts over all 44 stories).
- Navy UTC timeline strip: 22:52 Ulleung, 00:30 Japan PMI, 01:15 MNF,
  12:30 U-17, 17:45 Galatasaray, 18:00 Treasury, 20:00 UCL night,
  TUE Swiss row.
- Three lead stories with professional AI photographs (wire-service
  quality, no visible AI artefacts), each with a red banner, a red
  dateline, a headline, a short summary, a named source and a
  certainty tag (REPORTED / CLAIM / FORECAST).
- Twelve small stories, each with a flat-vector AI illustration
  (cream background, navy/red/gold palette, no text), a red banner,

## Date discipline

Two market numbers that circulated on Tuesday (Nikkei 60,279 and
Kospi 5,219) were dropped: they contradict the verified Friday close
of 56,436.65 and match a 10 Nov rebound story instead. The market
lead uses the verified Tuesday print: Nikkei futures 58,110 (+3%).

The DATE CHECK box lists, as not today: COP31 collapse (Sun), Hormuz
tankers mined (Sun), Nigeria boat, 24 dead (Fri), Bahrain IRGC attack
(Sat), Meldrum dies, 83 (Mon), Poland UAV (Mon night), NHS AI trial
(Mon), UK Women's World Cup 2035 (Mon). These are 20–23 Nov events
that a reader might expect to see; the box shows they were checked
and deliberately excluded.

Certainty tags: REPORTED = carried by the day's wires; CLAIM = one
side's account; FORECAST = still to happen when drawn.

## The map

"WHERE IT HAPPENED" uses real land outlines, not a dotted
approximation: Natural Earth 110m land polygons, simplified to about
1.5 degrees (Douglas-Peucker), with Antarctica dropped because it
sits below the map's latitude range (78 to −58). 70 polygons, 583
points, embedded in `gen_image.py` as `LAND_POLYS` and drawn filled
in a muted blue on the navy map panel. The 15 numbered pins are
placed by latitude/longitude with a small collision-avoidance pass,
so no two pins overlap.

## Files

- `main.py` — the entrypoint. Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network.
  Copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
- `gen_image.py` — regenerates `entry.png` deterministically from
  the assets in `build/`. Fixed fonts (DejaVu), fixed layout, no
  timestamps, no randomness, no network at runtime. Two runs are
  byte-identical. It hard-fails on any layout violation: a line wider
  than its allotted width, wrapped text over its line count, or a
  block bottom past its limit.
- `entry.png` — the built front page, 1024×1024, 518,652 bytes
  (under the 1 MiB cap).
- `build/assets/` — the 3 AI lead photographs (WebP).
- `build/illustrations/` — the 12 AI flat-vector illustrations
  (WebP) plus one unused oil illustration.
- `.makefun/` — the make.fun anchor (`freshness.json`) and the
  owner wallet (`owner.json`).

## Run

Regenerate the image:

```
python3 gen_image.py
```

This writes `entry.png` in this directory. Verify determinism by
running it twice and comparing SHA-256 hashes; both runs must be
byte-identical.

Run the entrypoint (as the harness does):

```
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```
